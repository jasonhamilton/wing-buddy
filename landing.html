How it works

  1. Describe the wing

    Add panels. Set length, root and tip chord, sweep, dihedral, twist and airfoil for each.

  2. Check it in 3D

    Ribs, spars, webbing and edges build live. Turn parts on and off, and flip to the planform or summary.

  3. Print and cut

    Export rib templates as a 1:1 PDF, or a DXF for your cutter. Both are in real units.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no paid tier.

Where are my designs stored?

In your browser's storage on your computer, and in project files you export. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I bring my own airfoils?

Yes. Import a .dat file and it is saved inside the project, so it travels with it. A starter set of NACA 4-digit airfoils is built in.

Can I open projects from other wing tools?

Not yet. Wing Buddy reads and writes its own project file.

What is not here yet?

Sheeting, rib combs, lightening holes, CNC and hot-wire exports and the airfoil designer are not built. Wing Buddy covers the core loop: panels, ribs, spars, 3D, PDF and DXF.
